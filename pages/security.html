How We Keep Your Data Secure

💻

Local Processing

All file conversions happen directly in your browser. Your documents never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.

🚫

No Server Upload

Unlike other services, we never upload your files to our servers. This eliminates the risk of data breaches and unauthorized access.

🔒

Secure Authentication

We use industry-standard OAuth 2.0 and magic link authentication to protect your account access.

🗑️

Automatic Data Clearing

All processed data is automatically cleared from browser memory when you close your session or navigate away.

🌐

HTTPS Encryption

All communications with our website are encrypted using TLS 1.3, the latest security protocol.

🔍

Regular Security Audits

We conduct regular security assessments and code reviews to identify and address potential vulnerabilities.

Security Details

1

Client-Side Processing

We leverage modern browser technologies like Web Workers and WebAssembly to process your files entirely on your device, ensuring your sensitive data never travels over the internet.

2

Minimal Data Collection

We only collect essential analytics data to improve our service. We never collect, store, or analyze the contents of your converted files.

3

Open Source Libraries

We use well-audited, open-source libraries for file processing. All libraries are regularly updated to patch any security vulnerabilities.

Compliance & Standards

FlashConverter is built with compliance in mind. While your data never reaches our servers, our website and authentication system follow industry best practices:

GDPR Compliant

We respect your privacy rights under the General Data Protection Regulation.

CCPA Compliant

We comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act requirements.

SOC 2 Type II

Our infrastructure partners maintain SOC 2 Type II certification for data security.

Security Incident Response

In the unlikely event of a security incident, we have established procedures to:

  • Identify and contain the incident within 1 hour of detection
  • Notify affected users within 72 hours
  • Conduct a thorough investigation and implement remediation measures
  • Provide transparent communication throughout the process
  • Publish post-incident reports for significant events
